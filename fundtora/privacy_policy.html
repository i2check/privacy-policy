Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026 | Version: 2.1 (Full Ecosystem Update)

At Fundtora, we operate under a strict "Privacy by Design" philosophy. This document outlines how your data is handled across our banking, vehicle management, and AI-driven features.

1. Data Collection Categories

To provide a comprehensive financial experience, we collect and process:

  • Identity & Account Data: Basic profile information (name and email address) retrieved via Google Sign-In to facilitate secure authentication and account personalization.
  • Financial Ecosystem Data: Manual bank account details, transaction history, intra-account transfers, category-specific budget limits, savings goals (stored in virtual vaults), and loan/debt repayment schedules.
  • Advanced Vehicle Data: Mileage/odometer logs, fuel and charging records (volume, cost, and efficiency), service history, and maintenance reminders (calculated via both time and distance thresholds).
  • AI Interaction Metadata: Context from your conversations with the AI Advisor and abstracted financial summaries used to generate health scores, executive reports, and predictive spending insights.
  • Security & Session Data: PIN hashes (never plaintext), biometric enrollment status, security logs (IP address, login times), and device metadata (Device UID, Model, OS Version) for session management and "Authorized Devices" features.
  • Subscription Telemetry: Purchase tokens and entitlement status processed via RevenueCat to manage access to Premium features.

2. Data Security & AI Ethics

We treat your sensitive information with bank-grade security protocols:

  • Local-First Encryption: Your primary financial data is stored in a secure SQLite database on your device, encrypted with AES-256.
  • Google Drive Private Sync: Optional cloud synchronization utilizes your personal Google Drive storage. We use the drive.appdata scope, meaning the app can only access its own private configuration folder and cannot view or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • AI Anonymization: Financial data is abstracted and anonymized before being processed by our reasoning engines (Google Gemini Pro) to ensure your identity remains private.

3. Third-Party Partners

We only share necessary data with trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google (Firebase & Drive API): For security logs, app analytics, and encrypted cloud synchronization via the restricted drive.appdata scope.
  • Google (Gemini Pro): For high-level financial reasoning, AI advice, and data abstraction.
  • RevenueCat: For secure subscription validation and Premium membership management.

4. Your Data Rights

Fundtora gives you full sovereignty over your information:

  • Full Data Portability: You can export all your records (Transactions, Vehicle Logs, Budgets) in Excel or PDF formats at any time.
  • The "Right to be Forgotten": You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud records via the app settings. This action is irreversible.
  • Remote Session Control: Through Device Management, you can remotely sign out any unauthorized or old devices.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or our data processing practices, please reach out via the Support Center or email us at privacy@fundtora.com.
