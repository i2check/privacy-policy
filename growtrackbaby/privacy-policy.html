Privacy Policy for GrowTrack Baby

Last updated: March 11, 2025

Welcome to GrowTrack Baby. Your privacy and the security of your baby's data are of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information within the application.

1. Information We Collect

GrowTrack Baby is designed to help you monitor your baby's growth and development. The application processes data that you enter manually, including:

  • Baby Profile: Name, date of birth, and gender.
  • Growth Data: Weight, height, and head circumference.
  • Health & Medical: Immunization records, sickness history, medications, and body temperature.
  • Daily Activities: Meal schedules (BLW/MPASI), milk intake (Feeding), and sleep patterns.
  • Development: Baby milestone achievements and parenting notes.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data you enter is used exclusively to provide the application's core features, such as:

  • Generating growth monitoring charts and statistics.
  • Providing personalized parenting guides based on your baby's age.
  • Tracking health history and daily routines.
  • Offering nutrition summaries and sleep quality insights.

3. Data Storage (Offline First)

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not own or maintain servers to store your personal information. Your data remains on your phone unless you choose to use the backup feature.

4. Backup & Sync (Google Drive)

If you voluntarily enable the Backup & Restore feature:

  • Your data will be uploaded to your own personal Google Drive account.
  • This data is stored in a private folder accessible only by the app using your credentials.
  • Data security on Google Drive is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and your account security settings.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Since the data is stored locally or on your personal cloud storage, we do not have access to it.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard practices to ensure that the application handles your data securely on your device. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We recommend securing your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer via email.
